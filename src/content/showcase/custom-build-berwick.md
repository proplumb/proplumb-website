This bespoke architectural residence in Berwick is a highly customised, high-end home defined by complex wet areas, premium materials, and detailed architectural features.

The build required early coordination around client-selected fittings and finishes, including custom marble throughout bathrooms and kitchen spaces.

The level of customisation meant plumbing and drainage works needed to be planned precisely from the outset, with little margin for error as the build progressed.
This project represents the type of high-end residential work where organisation, foresight, and disciplined execution are essential to achieving the intended architectural outcome.
