## Medium Density Plumbing Delivery

## Project Overview

This project supported a medium density residential development where plumbing works needed to align with repeatable build stages, shared service zones, and active site coordination.

## Scope Delivered

- Rough-in and fit-off plumbing across multiple dwellings
- Coordination of shared services and staged connections
- Support for site supervisors through clear scheduling and communication
- Quality checks to maintain consistency across repeated dwelling types

## Result

ProPlumb's structured delivery helped maintain momentum across the program while reducing rework and supporting predictable handovers.
