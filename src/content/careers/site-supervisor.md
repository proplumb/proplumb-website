## About the role

As a Site Supervisor, you will oversee daily operations across multiple active work fronts. This role requires practical leadership, strong communication, and a disciplined approach to planning and execution.

## What you will do

- Coordinate labour, materials, and subcontractor activity.
- Drive quality checks and compliance across all scopes.
- Manage daily progress reporting and site communication.
- Resolve coordination issues before they impact schedule.

## What we are looking for

- Demonstrated leadership experience in construction environments.
- Strong understanding of plumbing and civil sequencing.
- Confident communication with builders and site teams.
- Strong safety mindset and accountability.
