## About the role

We are looking for a qualified plumber who can take ownership of tasks on site and contribute to reliable program delivery. You will work with supervisors and project teams to ensure quality, compliance, and productivity targets are met.

## What you will do

- Complete rough-in, stack work, and fit-off to specification.
- Coordinate with site supervisors and other trades.
- Identify and resolve issues early to protect program timelines.
- Maintain accurate records and quality standards.

## What we are looking for

- Relevant plumbing qualification and licensing.
- Proven site experience across residential projects.
- Strong communication and team mindset.
- Commitment to safety and professional standards.
