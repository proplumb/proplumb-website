## About the role

As a Plumbing Apprentice at ProPlumb, you will work alongside experienced tradespeople across residential and development sites. You will gain practical exposure to rough-in, fit-off, and site coordination while building strong technical and professional habits.

## What you will do

- Support day-to-day plumbing activities under supervision.
- Assist with setup, material handling, and site preparation.
- Learn to interpret plans and follow installation sequences.
- Maintain safe work practices and clean work areas.

## What we are looking for

- Strong work ethic and willingness to learn.
- Reliable attendance and clear communication.
- Respect for safety processes and team accountability.
- Current driver's licence (or ability to obtain one soon).
