## About the role

This role supports our field teams by strengthening compliance systems and safety performance across projects. You will work closely with supervisors and leadership to maintain practical, site-ready standards.

## What you will do

- Conduct site audits and support corrective actions.
- Maintain records, registers, and compliance documentation.
- Assist with toolbox talks and safety communication.
- Identify trends and recommend practical improvements.

## What we are looking for

- Experience in construction compliance or safety roles.
- Strong documentation and reporting capabilities.
- Ability to communicate clearly with site and office teams.
- Practical, proactive approach to risk management.
